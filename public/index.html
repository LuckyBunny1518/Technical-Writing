PROJECT SUMMARY

What is this Research About?

A quick look at the central questions behind this study by Kabir.

Cognitive Offloading

When we know answers are instantly available via AI, our brains tend to offload remembering technical details and syntax, trusting the tool to recall it later.

Depth vs. Instant Summaries

Before AI, students explored documentation, textbooks, and diverse forums. Today, instant summaries save time but may reduce deep conceptual exploration.

The Productive Struggle

Struggling through complex technical problems builds strong mental connections. This study examines if AI shortcuts are reducing our problem-solving stamina.
